TEMPORAL AI CHALLENGE - Give AI a Sense of Time 🧠📈

LLMs read text. The real world speaks in signals: vitals, sensors, markets, grids. This weekend, you'll build AI that understands how the world changes over time.

🎯 The Mission 

Use Aionic's TimeNet to prepare open-source time-series data, then train a TSLM (Time-Series Language Model) that connects temporal signals with language for a  useful task.

Think: a patient's condition changing 🏥, a machine starting to fail 🏭, energy demand shifting ⚡ anywhere people act on signals.

🛠️ From Problem to Proof — 4 Steps 

1️⃣ PROBLEM + DATA — pick a target user & problem, source open data (agentic dataset-hunting pipelines encouraged!) 

2️⃣ CONNECT — build/use a TimeNet connector for signals, metadata & annotations. Reusable ingestion pipelines welcome!

3️⃣ TRAIN + EVALUATE — train or fine-tune a TSLM, compare vs. baseline on held-out data (no leakage across time/subjects/devices!) 

4️⃣ DEMONSTRATE — real inputs, model outputs, and how it helps your user. Show evidence and limitations.
